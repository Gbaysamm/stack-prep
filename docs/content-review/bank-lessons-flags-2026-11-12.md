# Question-bank lessons: possible content errors for a teacher (12 Nov 2026)

Raised by the language editors (docs/questions/bank-editor-brief.md) while rewording every
spoken line of the past-question lessons. Nothing here was changed: each is a teacher's call.
Fix at the source (the lessons_YYYY.yaml file) and reload the subject.

## accounts/lessons_2010.yaml

- Q12: The say line says 'The bank charged a small rate on the total of the withdrawals', but the later line gives 'five naira for every one thousand naira'. These agree, but the claim that the Central Bank phased C O T out and replaced it with a capped account maintenance fee should be checked by a teacher.

## Outcome (13 Nov 2026)

Every flag was checked against its question (stem, options, checked key) by the teaching
pass and a follow-up: 64 fixed in the lessons, 78 found not to be errors (some checked
twice), none an answer-key problem. Each verdict and what was done:

- **accounts/lessons_2012.yaml Q18** (fixed): The flag was right. The 'In Nigeria' example said money the barber earned was simply 'recorded as expenses', which read as if a cost had been booked as a cost. I checked it against the stem (revenue of 15,000 recorded as an expense) and explanations_2012 (answer B, 30,000). I rewrote the example as income from a course he ran that was wrongly recorded as an expense, and added a moment that ties it to the profit being too low by 30,000, not 15,000. I also split the rule into its two effects (charged as an expense, left out of revenue), each worth 15,000. The answer is still B.
- **accounts/lessons_2012.yaml Q50** (not an error): Section 86 of the 1999 Constitution says the President appoints the Auditor-General for the Federation, on the recommendation of the Federal Civil Service Commission and subject to confirmation by the Senate. That matches the lesson and key B. Trap D's 'Civil Service Commission' is the option's wording for the same Federal Civil Service Commission, and it rightly says that body recommends the candidate but does not appoint. Nothing changed.
- **accounts/lessons_2013.yaml Q14** (fixed): Confirmed. The stem says the shop bought from Freehold Enterprises, but the lesson never introduced the name, yet the double-entry line and traps C and D used it. The question moment now reads 'buys mattresses to sell from a supplier, Freehold Enterprises, and pays three hundred and five thousand, one hundred and fifty naira by cheque'. The Freehold sentence moved out of the 'Cr Bank' moment into its own moment ('Freehold paid at once ⟹ not a creditor'), and the Cr Bank line now gives its reason. The answer is still B, Dr Purchases and Cr Bank.
- **accounts/lessons_2013.yaml Q24** (fixed): Confirmed. The spoken rule had '+ profit' but the board showed '− Loss' with no profit term. Now one moment states the general rule, Closing = Opening + Additional + Profit − Drawings, with the matching board line.
