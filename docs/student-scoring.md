# How students are scored

Status: **the rule, version 3.1 (12 Oct 2026).** This replaces every earlier draft. Every screen
that shows a number is checked against this page.

**The test:** any student can explain their own score to a friend.

---

## 1. Topic score

> **Topic score = questions right ÷ questions answered in that topic.**

- If you redo a question, **your latest answer replaces the old one.** Fixing a mistake
  raises the score.
- A right answer only counts if you got it without the hint or the board, and didn't tap "I
  guessed". Otherwise the question comes back later (section 5).

Example: Vowels, 10 questions, 2 right = **20%**. You redo the same 10 and get 3 right:
Vowels = 3 of 10 = **30% (+10)**.

| Topic score | Label |
|---|---|
| Nothing answered | Not tried yet |
| Fewer than 3 answered | Just started |
| Under 50% | Needs work |
| 50–79% | Getting there |
| 80% and over | Strong |

## 2. Your score (one per subject)

> **Your score = the average of your topic scores** (the topics you've tried).

Example:
- Vowels 20% and Stress 40% → your score = (20 + 40) ÷ 2 = **30%**.
- Practise Vowels up to 60% → your score = (60 + 40) ÷ 2 = **50% (+20)**.

## 3. Check-ups

A check-up is a short mixed test with no help, like the exam.

- **The first check-up comes first.** Practice opens only after it.
- **A check-up is due every 7 days.** When one is due, practice is locked until it's taken.
- **A check-up's result** is the average of the topic scores from that check-up alone. Its
  answers also count in your topic scores, like any other answers.
- **Started at** = your first check-up's result.
- **Predicted score** = your latest check-up's result. Each check-up is a checkpoint, so the
  history shows the trend: "30% → 38% → 47%".
- **Which questions:** questions you haven't seen yet come first. If a topic has none left,
  it reuses the ones you saw longest ago.

## 3a. Mock exams are practice, not check-ups

A mock exam is practice under exam conditions: the full timed paper in the CBT room, with its
own report.
- It **never** sets the predicted score, never counts as a check-up and never unlocks
  practice. Only check-ups do those.
- **Before the first check-up nothing is scored,** not even a mock's answers. The scores start
  at the first check-up.
- **After that, a mock's answers count in the topic scores** like practice answers, and its
  wrong questions come back in practice.

## 4. What the student sees

- **Home:** your score with "+N since you started at X%", your predicted score, your target,
  and when the next check-up is due.
- **After each practice run:** your score before → after, and each topic you practised, e.g.
  "Vowels 20% → 30% (+10)".
- **After each check-up:** its result, the change since the last check-up, and the weakest
  topics to practise.

## 5. Questions come back until you get them

A question comes back in a later practice run (never the same run) when you got it wrong,
were taught it on the board, or guessed it. About one question in three of a run is one of
these, marked "Back again". Get it right and the topic score goes up.

## 6. Behind the scenes (never shown as numbers)

- **Harder when it's too easy:** in each topic, a right answer moves the next question up a
  level (1 very easy … 5 very hard), and a wrong answer moves it down one.
- **What to practise first:** weak topics that come up most in the exam. Topics at 80% or
  more (with 5 or more answered) go to the back.
